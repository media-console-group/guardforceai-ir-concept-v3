Platform and how it works

An AI platform, industry agents and robots as a service.

“Our AI solutions are built on three interconnected layers.” Company site The 20-F dates the first: “The Guardforce AI Intelligent Cloud Platform (GFAI ICP) forms a foundation of our AI&Robotics Solution since its launch in 2021.” Company statement 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

  1. AI infrastructureIntelligent Cloud Platform (ICP)

    Connects the company’s AI agents and robots into one system that routes data and actions across deployments. Company site

  2. Multi-agent systemIndustry-specialized AI agents

    The site names travel, retail, education and banking. Company site The filed example is DVGO (DeepVoyage Go), a travel-planning agent: beta in April 2025, public in January 2026, sold by subscription. Filed 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

  3. Robotics as a serviceSale and rental of robots

    Robots sold outright or rented with technical support, including Wishnote for event sign-in and wish displays. Filed 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

The established base

Founded in 1982, serving Thailand’s banks and retailers.

“GF Cash (CIT), Guardforce AI’s legacy business, was incorporated in 1982 in Thailand and has been operating in the cash-in-transit, or CIT, industry since inception.” Company statement 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

The listed company is a Cayman Islands holding company formed on April 20, 2018 to acquire that business; its principal office moved from Bangkok to Singapore in November 2021. The Thai subsidiary now trades under the GFCS brand. Filed 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

Meeting, Oct 27, 2026

A 1-for-40 consolidation and a dual-class structure

Resolution 1 consolidates every 40 shares into one, then reduces par value to US$0.001. Resolution 2 creates Class A shares with one vote and Class B shares with 20; the 80,034 post-consolidation shares of the CEO and Chair, Lei Wang, would become Class B. It also raises the authorised share count from 300,000,000 to 36,000,000,000 while 799,233 shares would be in issue. Resolution 3 moves the equity plan limit from 15% of all ordinary shares to 20% of Class A shares. Resolutions 1 and 2 need a two-thirds vote. Filed 6-K proxy, Oct 2, 2026 The company made an earlier 1-for-40 consolidation in 2023. Filed 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

Computed from the proxy table, not stated in it: 80,034 Class B shares at 20 votes would carry about 69.0% of the votes on about 10.0% of the 799,233 post-consolidation shares. Computed The 20-F also records an irrevocable proxy for Ms. Wang over 2,947,150 other shares, not counted in that figure. Filed 20-F, Apr 21, 2026

Control and pay

A rights plan and a CEO consulting agreement

A shareholder rights plan adopted Dec 15, 2025 dilutes anyone who acquires 10% or more of the voting power without Board approval; founders have an initial 25% exempted percentage. Filed 6-K, Dec 19, 2025 The CEO’s consulting agreement, approved Sep 15, 2025, pays an annual cash fee of US$650,000 plus share awards and allowances; termination without cause pays a lump sum of five years’ fee and target bonus, with tax gross-up payments.
